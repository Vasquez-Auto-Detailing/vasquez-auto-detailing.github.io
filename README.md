# Vasquez Auto Detailing

Standalone, static website for the Vasquez Auto Detailing business in Fontana, California.

## Preview

Run the site through a local web server and open `index.html`. The portfolio is populated from `portfolio.js`, so use a server rather than opening the file directly.

## Publishing

The `Deploy static site to GitHub Pages` workflow publishes the site from the `main` branch. The root `.gitignore` excludes original camera-roll exports; optimized website media in `assets/` is included.

## Business identity and prices

The website uses the supplied original Vasquez logo in the header and hero, with its matching black, blue, and white color palette. Source logo and promotional artwork remain unchanged. Listed prices, add-ons, and package descriptions reflect the supplied Vasquez service information; vehicle-specific estimates and final inclusions should be confirmed with the business. The site identifies Fontana as the service location and notes that prices may vary by vehicle size and condition.

## Media

`portfolio.js` lists the selected vehicle and interior photos and videos with visual descriptions, accessible labels, and category tags. Media captions are not displayed on gallery cards or in the full-size viewer; descriptive text remains available to assistive technology. Captions and categories are matched to the pictured media, and branding artwork is excluded from the work gallery. The gallery has vehicle, interior, and hands-on detailing-clip filters; selecting a card opens an accessible full-size viewer, where visitors can navigate items in that category. The homepage also includes a step-by-step estimate guide and FAQs about mobile service, pricing, and contacting the business. Its background video rotates through selected vehicle and detailing clips, pauses when the page is hidden, and stays on a poster for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Supplied WebP pictures, video posters, and browser-compatible H.264 MP4 clips are stored in `assets/`; they were generated from the originals. Exact OneDrive duplicate copies appear once. Original photos, logos, flyers and MOV videos are not changed or deleted.

Before-and-after comparisons are not labeled because the photo and video collection does not establish any same-vehicle, matched before/after pairs. In particular, no clean photo could be confidently matched to the supplied back-seat photo, so that photo is omitted rather than shown without a verified clean result. Branding images and promotional graphics are excluded from the work gallery. Pricing and package information is presented directly in the service cards.

## Contact and services

The newer supplied Instagram screenshots show the business phone number, Instagram account, service list, and service areas. The site links directly to call or text `(909) 328-9499` and to `@vasquez.auto.detailing`, and identifies the Inland Empire, Los Angeles, and Orange County as service areas. Foam wash, paint correction, and ceramic coating are included as estimate-request options; the service flyer does not list prices for those options. The quote form copies a request to the visitor's clipboard and does not claim to send it automatically.
