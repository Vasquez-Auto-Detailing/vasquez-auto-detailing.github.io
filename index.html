GOOD TO KNOW

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS.

What affects the final price?

Vehicle size and condition can affect pricing. Extra-heavy dirt, stains, pet hair, or additional labor may change the estimate, so share those details when you reach out.

Does Vasquez come to me?

Vasquez is a mobile detailing business based in Fontana. Share your location when requesting an estimate to confirm service availability.

How do I send the estimate request?

The form prepares your details to copy and paste into a text or email. You can also call or text Vasquez directly at (909) 328-9499.

What details should I include?

Include your vehicle’s year, make, model, its general condition, the service you’re interested in, and any add-ons you’d like to ask about.
